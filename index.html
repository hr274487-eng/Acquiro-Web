Operational Flow

From the first ring to confirmed action.

Every call follows a structured, verified pathway configured specifically for your business operations.

Step 01

Answer

Answers promptly 24/7 with a natural, conversational voice. Greets callers and addresses common questions using your approved business knowledge base.

Step 02

Capture

Gathers caller contact details, identifies the exact nature of their enquiry, and asks qualifying questions tailored to your service criteria.

Step 03

Book or Route

Checks live calendar availability to book appointments directly, or transfers urgent calls to designated human staff according to your transfer rules.

Step 04

Update

Synchronizes call records with your CRM, Google Sheets, or Airtable. Dispatches instant confirmation emails and notifies your team via SMS or internal alerts.

How We Work

A structured, careful process from planning to production.
